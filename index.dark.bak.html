Security & trust

Guardrails before the first query runs.

Your data is isolated, every query is checked before it runs, and access stays locked to your account.

01

Tenant isolation

Every account gets its own Postgres schema. Your data lives in its own schema and is not stored in shared tables with other accounts.

02

Validated SQL

A validation layer inspects every query before it runs and blocks anything that writes or modifies data. Queries run only inside your own schema.

03

Authenticated access

Token-based authentication with bcrypt-hashed passwords. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short answers to what small-business owners ask before their first upload.

Do I need to know SQL or build formulas?
No. You ask questions in plain English and an agent writes and validates the SQL for you. There are no formulas, pivot tables, or query languages to learn.
What file formats can I upload?
CSV and Excel (XLSX) files, the sales and purchase exports you already keep. You can upload them directly or sync them from a Google Drive folder, and the structure is inferred on ingest.
Is my data private and isolated from other accounts?
Yes. Each account gets its own isolated Postgres schema, so your data is not stored in shared tables with other accounts. Access is protected by token authentication and bcrypt-hashed passwords.
How fast are answers?
Seconds. You ask a question and the response streams back live as the agent works, returning a grounded number, a short narrative, and the right chart without waiting on a report cycle.
How do I know the numbers are correct?
Every answer is grounded in real rows from your own uploaded data, not generated from guesswork. The agent writes SQL, validates it before running, and blocks any query that would write or modify data. Queries execute only inside your account's isolated schema.
Can I use it on my own data without a team setup?
Yes. Each account is a single owner account with its own isolated Postgres schema. You sign in, connect your files, and ask questions against your own data — no team configuration or seat management to set up.
